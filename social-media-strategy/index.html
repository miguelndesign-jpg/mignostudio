HOW WE DESIGNED ADS WITH THE right energy

We created content that captured the feeling of relaxation, friendship, and escaping daily stress.

The key wasn’t selling surf classes, it was selling the benefits of our client's business: connection, fun, and a community. People became fans, bought packs, and stayed long-term.

We achieved this with a multi-disciplinary approach of combining Social Media Marketing, Brand Strategy, and Graphic Design.

HOW WE DESIGNED ADS
TO SELL AND BUILD COMMUNITY,
WITHOUT A PR MANAGER

You should plan first, research who your customers are, and then design the ads.

For example, we used Kodak and Polaroid effects, newspaper textures, and other symbols of an offline lifestyle. Our research showed that’s what our customers wanted, so that’s the kind of graphics they react to while scrolling through social media.

Check the notes below to learn more about making design choices that support your business and its goals.

THE SECRET FORMULA

  • By using images of people with their backs turned or under shadows, viewers can more easily imagine themselves at the event.

  • Most surf schools miss this: they have a lot to offer but don't show it. We listed everything included in the event.
    Example: a surf suit, a locker to store your things, a surfboard, a surf lesson, and more. It will help answer questions before people ask them and sell the fact that a lot is included in this event.

  • We added "good vibes included" to the list, because it's important to add humour while we are informing people. The best marketing is a mix of humour and useful information.

  • Our strategy was to use newspaper textures, retro photography, and other symbols that communicate the message of an offline lifestyle, which is what our customers are interested in.

  • We are selling an offline lifestyle, good vibes, and friendship, but via surfing, which is more effective than just selling a surf lesson.

  • Adding a line stating that "all skill levels are welcome" will bring people who have never surfed before, and we can convert them to long-term customers who buy surf packs and other surf trips.

  • You'd be surprised, but many surf businesses miss adding critical details like event information, school location, meeting times, and how to register.

    Probably because you, as a business owner, are busy and spread yourself thin, at least that's what we found with Kiber Surf School to be the case.

HOW TO GET
MORE FOLLOWERS
WHO WANT TO BUY FROM YOU

The first challenge was figuring out who would buy from us.

I used a “persona profile map” for this; a method successful companies like Apple use before creating new products.

For example, Apple makes a persona profile to see if there’s a market and enough people who would buy the iPod before they build it. This ensures the product will succeed and lets them improve it before launch.

I used the same method for this Surf School client.

PLAN MARKETING AND BRAND STRATEGY
WITHOUT DOUBLE THE WORK

Good graphic design is critical for grabbing people's attention, making them stop scrolling and click on your post. You can evoke a reaction in people by using specific colors, patterns, textures, and fonts that convey the message your customers respond to, without using a single word. This is the fastest and most efficient way to advertise your business.

It was also the perfect opportunity to create a campaign that would help define a Brand while delivering new Graphic Design content for the surf school to use in later posts.

OUTSMART YOUR COMPETITION

This step plans your marketing campaign while you define your brand strategy and know exactly what graphic designs you will need for the future, without having to hire more graphic designers or consultants or spend more money on revisions. After this research, you will already know what will look best and what gets you results.

We selected a few symbols to reuse, making new content fast, consistent, and recognizable by our followers. They’d recognize our content before they even see who posted it, helping to make our client’s brand memorable.

HOW WE PLANNED OUR POSTS FOR IMPACT

This is how we planned the timing of our posts on social media to get the most out of our campaigns. We designed them to generate hype, inform, raise engagement, and urgency about the client’s business.

We organized our posts using the Notion Calendar app.

  • Step 1 — Hype Your Business

    Post a short teaser to hint that something’s coming, without promising it.

    For this example, I asked our Instagram: “Surf or Skate?” Our followers were struggling to choose between the two, then we revealed an event with both!

  • Step 2 — Share your Event

    This is the information phase.

    After some time has passed, make your ad, and post it on social media.

  • Step 3 — Inspire People to Join

    We made a trailer for our event that would act as an ad and as a testimonial.

    We showcased all our activities, from surfing and taking breaks during surf trips to relaxing at a lake, visiting local markets, and anything else that inspires people to join in the adventure and shares the benefits of the client’s business.

    We also learned that mixing video reels and image ads would maximize our reach with the Instagram algorithm.

  • Step 4 — Befriend Your Customers

    Share skate tricks, beginner surf tips, memes, and other fun content in your Social Media stories. It shows that your business has an easy-going personality.

    Many content creators already make this kind of content, so you can repost it for free and reach more people.

    People relate to personalities, not shops or products.

  • Step 5 — Remind Them of You

    As the week goes on, followers may get distracted. Post a second, shorter ad with key event info; this can also bring in last-minute sign-ups and reach even more people!

  • Step 6 — Make it Feel Urgent!

    Post a short video reel, showing your existing customers having fun in your surf classes, while reminding them that the time to sign up is coming to an end.

    This will inform, inspire, engage, build community, and remind them to sign up. You should also add a "Questions and Answers" poll across your social media. Many customers sign up during this phase, especially introverts! You don’t want to skip it!

  • Step 7 — Report

    We found that sharing photos and videos of the session immediately afterward helps those who didn't attend feel like they missed out. This can increase their interest in coming to the next session!

OUR CAMPAIGN
SCRIPT

”Aloha, children of the sea and the sun!

Join us for a super sunny Surf + Skate day on Sunday, 18th June:

  • Opening Circle: Kiber Surf School. 2PM+ (free snacks!)

  • Surf Session: 2:45 PM+

  • Skate Session: The garden behind the school, at 5PM+

  • Closing Circle: TBA!

This session is a perfect weekend event for an intensive and beginner-friendly session, and for anyone who wants to get started in surfing and skateboarding.

In a very short time, you'll meet like-minded people who are also looking for new aspirations, connections, and aren't scared of trying new stuff.

Link to sign up is in Bio, and you can find more info: your-site-link

Or directly reach out to our Surf School on Instagram: @your-school

Or ring: +351 1234567 for any questions, asking to borrow a skateboard, in case you don't have one, and want to try. Spots and boards are limited, so hurry up and save your spot before the event.

While the surf requires sign-up, the skate part of the event is free and requires no registration; just show up!”

Tip: Make it easy for visitors to register by sharing all the information they need.

Always end your posts with a strong call to action, like: “Call us now to get started!” or "Link in bio to sign up!"

This is important because without a call to action, people will get distracted and scroll to the next cat video on Instagram instead of signing up right away.
